## For StorageHub

The round-4 list's own items still hold, and the docs now say them: use stable connection ids (plans and
stored jobs bind to them), a global fencing counter and revisions that continue across re-adding in its
store, keep the store open until `DisposeAsync` returns, bound pause/cancel/remove (now `ControlTimeout`,
30 s by default), and give the service and the desktop different `WorkerId`s.
